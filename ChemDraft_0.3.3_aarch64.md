Charge marks, electron-pushing mechanism arrows, and a Copy As menu for chemistry formats.

- **Charge marks:** anions and cations are now movable marks that associate with the nearest atom — place one from the charge dropdown and the atom takes the charge; drag the mark away and the atom reverts to its neutral valence instead of showing a stuck error. Marks ride along when their molecule is dragged, rotated, flipped, or scaled, so a repositioned structure keeps its chemistry.
- **Mechanism arrows:** ChemDraw-style electron-pushing arrows (full- and half-headed) that anchor to atoms or charge marks and gain one or two Bézier handles by length. They replace the old rotate-into-circle curved arrows.
- **Symbol tools:** charge, radical-dot, and lone-pair tools live behind one dropdown, matching the ChemDraw palette.
- **Atom relabel:** the text tool can relabel an existing atom — hover, click, type the element, then Enter or click off.
- **Copy As:** a new submenu in the Edit menu and the object right-click for SMILES, InChI, InChI Key, CDXML, MOL V2000/V3000, SVG, and PNG. PNG copies render at 4× with embedded 288 dpi density metadata; SVG copies paste as real vector art in Illustrator instead of raw markup.
- **Clipboard fixes:** structure paste no longer corrupts the desktop clipboard, right-click no longer collapses a multi-object selection, and scoped copies keep their charges and mechanism arrows with unwarped arrow geometry.

Automatic updates are delivered via Sparkle (File ▸ Check for Updates…).
