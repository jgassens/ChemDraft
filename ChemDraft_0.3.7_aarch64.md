ChemDraft now runs on Windows, and large drawings are much faster to edit, save and reopen.

- **ChemDraft for Windows.** The first Windows release, for Windows 10 and 11 (x64). It installs per user with no administrator rights and updates itself after asking you first. Everything you know from the Mac version is there, with Ctrl-based shortcuts.
- **Large drawings stay responsive.** Rotating, moving, aligning or deleting thousands of selected objects now takes about as long per object as editing a few, rather than slowing down sharply as the selection grows. Saving is faster, undo history no longer grows without limit, and very large files open again after saving.
- **More of CDXML comes through.** Groups and dative bonds in CDXML files are kept, and a file that cannot be opened says why. Damaged or hostile CDXML files are refused safely instead of stalling the app.
- **Keyboard shortcuts fixed.** Group, Bring to Front, Bring Forward, Send Backward and Send to Back work from the keyboard again.
- **Plugins read labels as drawn.** Condensed labels such as OH and NH2 reach plugins (the NMR predictor included) the way they appear on the page.
- **Faster toolbars at launch.** The toolbars appear sooner after opening the app.

Automatic updates are delivered via Sparkle (File ▸ Check for Updates…).
