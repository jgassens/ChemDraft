# Approved journal catalog and provenance

The contribution includes **20 partial profiles**: five starter entries and fifteen in an optional
local data pack. Only categories with accessible official instructions and represented facts are
included. All check dates and versions below come from the actual profile data, not an invented
claim that every journal was checked today. Crossref supplies identity metadata only.

Click **Load additional journal pack (15 journals)** to load the optional local files. No startup
network request, database server or executable plugin installation is involved. Loading changes no drawing.

## Scope and important exceptions

- ACS entries independently cite each journal's instructions. Minimum type/line sizes are not exact
  bond-length mandates. Chemical Reviews' TOC size is not inherited by ACS Nano or ACS Catalysis.
- The five RSC entries independently cite their own chemical-structure/TOC sections. Published point
  values are used rather than converting their rounded centimetre equivalents.
- Each of the four Advanced journals has its own checked Graphics FAQ. Ambiguous spacing wording is
  withheld. Chemical line-art raster fallback is distinguished from microscopy-image guidance.
- Angewandte: the narrow TOC option is supported; the wide option is withheld because the
  [Graphics Guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15213773/homepage/graphics-guidelines)
  and [Notice to Authors](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15213773/homepage/notice-to-authors)
  disagree. Approximate text-size advice is not converted into an invented exact font-size rule.
- Nature Materials/Energy: only the shared
  [chemical-structure guide](https://www.nature.com/documents/nr-chemical-structures-guide.pdf), which
  explicitly covers Nature Research journals, is used. Unknown journal-specific figure limits,
  formats and DPI remain empty. Nature Chemistry's bounds are not copied to these entries.
- Molecules: export guidance only. Its recommended DPI is not claimed to be an acceptance threshold;
  the profile does not invent chemical appearance or borrow table-font settings for atom labels.
- Cell: profile 1.0.1 is enabled for final-production chemical figures, after the current
  [Cell submission page](https://www.cell.com/cell/information-for-authors/final-submission) and its
  [linked figure guide](https://www.cell.com/information-for-authors/figure-guidelines) loaded in the
  normal browser. The research fetcher had returned 403; that error alone was not verification.
  Article-layout widths and explicit chemical drawing settings are represented. Separate stereo
  width, label display, font embedding and generic-versus-chemical line-width differences are
  disclosed. Cell graphical abstracts and Leading Edge artwork are outside this profile.

All profiles preserve unknown settings and disclose renderer/override limits. A source-check date
is separate from checking a particular drawing. Review the final export at its actual physical size.
Only a small original transcription of facts and bibliographic metadata is bundled; no publisher
style-sheet files, templates, artwork or full author-guide text are redistributed.

## Omitted pending verification

Chemical Engineering Journal, Journal of Hazardous Materials, Applied Catalysis B: Environment and
Energy (requested under its former Environmental name), Nano Energy, and International Journal of
Biological Macromolecules are **absent from the selectable catalog and data pack**. Official guide
requests were inaccessible during this audit. This does not establish that they have no fixed rules.
They may be added later after individual source verification, rather than borrowing publisher defaults.
